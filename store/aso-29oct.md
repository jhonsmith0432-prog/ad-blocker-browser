## Research summary

**Play metadata rules (current):** title max 30 chars; no emoji, emoticons or repeated special characters in title/icon/developer name; no ALL CAPS unless brand; no store-performance or ranking claims ("#1", "Top", "Best of Play", "Popular"), no price/promo text ("10% off", "free for limited time") and no Google Play program references ("Editor's choice", "New"); avoid repetitive or unrelated keywords. Short description 80 chars, same bans plus no calls to action. Full description 4,000 chars, must describe the app, no unattributed testimonials. Google Play has no hidden keyword field; title is weighted most, then short description, then full; repeating title words in the short description wastes space. [1][2][3]

## Rule risks and things to confirm

- **VPN in the short description while it is Premium.** The full description labels it Premium; still, if a reviewer calls it misleading, use short option 2 or drop "and a VPN".
- **Data safety vs the Premium VPN.** `docs/DATA_SAFETY.md` answers "No data collected", but VPN users' browser traffic (and their IP) passes through Nexcraft servers (Vultr / OVH). Under Play's definitions data sent to the developer's servers is "collected" even if processed ephemerally, so the form likely needs an ephemeral declaration. The listing deliberately avoids "no data collected" and only says no telemetry, no analytics, no ads profile, history stays on the phone. Make sure the VPN servers keep no logs before claiming more.
- **Trial length.** Listing says a 3-day trial on yearly (per brief). `STATUS.md` placeholders mention 7-day trial offers. The text must match the Play offer exactly.
- **Third-party names.** uBlock Origin (one factual "powered by" line), Mozilla/Gecko (owner-approved line) and Global Privacy Control (a spec name) appear once each. No Firefox. If a reviewer flags impersonation, drop the uBlock line first.
- **Title edge cases.** de and tr titles are exactly 30. tr uses the verb "Reklam Engelle" (block ads); if read as a call to action, swap to "Reklam Engelleyici ve Tarayıcı" (30). "Ad Blocker" describes function, not "ad free", and eyeo's title shows it is accepted.
- **Russia.** Google Play billing for paid apps/subscriptions has been suspended in Russia since 2022, so ru users can browse but likely cannot buy Premium. Keep the ru listing for installs; check before spending on ru UA.
- **zh-CN.** Play does not operate in mainland China; this listing only reaches Simplified-Chinese users elsewhere (e.g. Malaysia, Singapore). Low priority.
- **Spanish locale.** Written for es-ES (vídeo, móvil); can be copied to es-419 as is (only spelling of "vídeo" differs).
- **Accent colors** spelled US-style for en-US.
