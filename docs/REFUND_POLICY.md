# Refund Policy: Ad Blocker & Private Browser

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Premium is sold through Google Play. Google charges you, and Google holds your payment details. We never see them.

## Free trial

New subscribers get a 3-day free trial. Cancel in Google Play before the trial ends and you will not be charged.

## Within 48 hours of a purchase

Ask Google directly: open [play.google.com](https://play.google.com) → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, pick the purchase and choose "Request a refund". Google decides these requests under the [Google Play refund policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/15574908).

## After 48 hours

Email us at **mohsindevelop.me@gmail.com** with your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA.") and the reason. We reply within 5 business days.

- **Subscriptions.** Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for. We do not normally refund cash for time already started. Instead we offer **Shield Credits** for the unused part of your period, which keep Premium active in the app.
- **Lifetime purchase.** If Premium does not work for you and we cannot fix it, we offer Shield Credits or decide on a refund case by case.
- **Charged by mistake or by fraud.** Tell us and we will refund through Google Play.

## Shield Credits

Shield Credits are the app's in-app credit. They are only issued by us, as a code sent by email, when you ask for a refund. They cannot be bought and are not earned through normal use.

- 1 credit = 1 day of Premium. Enter the code in the app: Premium → Shield Credits → Redeem code.
- Credits have no cash value, cannot be transferred or exchanged for money, and only work in this app.
- A code can be used once and must be redeemed within 60 days of being issued.
- Credits are stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them; email us and we will reissue the remaining days once.

## Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits rights you have under the consumer law of your country. If that law entitles you to a cash refund (for example for faulty digital content, or a withdrawal right that applies to you), we will give one.
