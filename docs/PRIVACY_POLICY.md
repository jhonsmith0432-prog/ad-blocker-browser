# Privacy Policy — Ad Blocker & Private Browser

_Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLISHING]_

Ad Blocker & Private Browser ("the app") is published by [DEVELOPER / COMPANY NAME] ("we").
This policy explains what happens to your data when you use the app.

## The short version

**We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal data.** We have no analytics, no
advertising SDKs, no user accounts of our own and no servers that receive your browsing data.

## What stays on your phone

Everything the app needs to work is stored only on your device:

- Your browsing history, bookmarks, open tabs, saved passwords and site settings
  (private tabs keep none of these and are wiped when closed).
- The count of ads and trackers blocked, shown in the app's stats.
- Your Premium status and app preferences.

You can delete all of it at any time in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

## Services the app connects to

A browser has to talk to the internet. These connections are made directly from your phone; we never
see them:

| Service | Why | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Websites you visit | To load the pages you ask for | Your normal web requests, as with any browser |
| Your chosen search engine (DuckDuckGo by default) | To run searches you type | Your search query |
| Ad-block filter list hosts (uBlock Origin lists) | To keep blocking rules up to date | A standard download request |
| Mozilla services used by the Firefox engine (e.g. security blocklists, extension updates, search configuration) | Security updates and browser features | Standard technical requests; see Mozilla's privacy notice |
| Google Safe Browsing (if enabled) | Warn you about dangerous sites | Partial hashes of URLs, as used by most browsers |
| Google Play Billing | Only if you buy Premium | Handled entirely by Google Play under Google's privacy policy |

Usage data ("telemetry") built into the Firefox engine is **switched off** in this app.

## Crash reports

If the app crashes it may ask whether to send a crash report to Mozilla. Nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone.
